# Optim-Cyb @ UCT Prague

Welcome to the **Optim-Cyb** GitHub organization. This space serves as the central repository hub for our research group at the **University of Chemistry and Technology, Prague (VŠCHT)**.

## 🔬 About Us
We focus on applied cybernetics, optimization, and advanced process control. This organization hosts:
* **Open Source Projects:** Tools and libraries developed during our research.
* **Research Repositories:** Private and public codebases related to our ongoing scientific work.
* **Educational Resources:** Materials for students and collaborators.
